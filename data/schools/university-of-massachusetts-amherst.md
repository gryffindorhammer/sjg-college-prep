## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics (College of Natural Sciences) offers a BS in Mathematics (six concentration options or self-designed) and a BS in Statistics and Data Science, plus a 5th-year BS/MS in statistics.

Source: [UMass Amherst Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.umass.edu/mathematics-statistics/academics/undergraduate) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theater offers a BA major (36+ credits: performance, design/production, directing, dramaturgy) and a 16-credit minor, plus a Multicultural Theater Certificate. No audition needed to declare either.

Source: [UMass Amherst Department of Theater](https://www.umass.edu/theater/theater-minor) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs
