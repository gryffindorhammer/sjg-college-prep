## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, and Statistics offers a BA/BS in Mathematics, a BS in Applied Mathematics, and a BA/BS in Statistics (actuarial-science concentration available).

Source: [Case Western Reserve Bulletin](https://bulletin.case.edu/arts-sciences/mams/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theater offers a BA major and an 18-credit-hour minor, with concentrations in acting, directing, dramatic writing, and design/technical theater. Scholarships awarded annually to both majors and minors.

Source: [Case Western Reserve Department of Theater](https://theater.case.edu/academic-programs/minor-in-theater/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

4 department productions/year at the historic Eldred Theater, plus two student-run groups outside the department season: CWRU Footlighters and Players' Theatre Group (PTG).

Source: [Case Western Reserve Department of Theater](https://theater.case.edu/theater-season-and-box-office/theater-season/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

'Freshmen and non-majors are allowed to perform in productions' — departmental productions are open to the entire university community, with unified auditions each semester.

Source: [Case Western Reserve Department of Theater](https://theater.case.edu/theater-season-and-box-office/theater-auditions/) · checked 2026-08-23
