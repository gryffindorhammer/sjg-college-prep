Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics and Statistics (Harpur College) offers a BA and BS in Mathematical Sciences, each with mathematics, actuarial science, or data-science/statistics tracks; the BS is more upper-level/proof-oriented and geared toward graduate study. Students must be admitted to Harpur College before declaring the major.Binghamton Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics — Undergraduate Program · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programBFA major (also offers a minor) — Dept. of Theatre offers a BFA in Musical Theatre — a four-year sequence building acting, singing, and dance skills — alongside a general BA in Theatre and a Theatre Minor (four non-practicum courses plus practicum credits).Binghamton Theatre Department — BFA Musical Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsFall Mainstage auditions run the first weekend of school; Spring production auditions run at the end of Fall semester. The department runs Mainstage, Studio, and Open Season productions each year, plus dance classes/productions through the School of the Arts.Binghamton Theatre Department — Auditions · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationAny registered student taking at least 4 credits may audition for any Theatre Department production (Mainstage, Studio, or Open Season) — majors and minors are not required. Dance classes and productions are likewise open to all registered students, and non-majors can also get involved backstage.Binghamton Theatre Department — Auditions FAQ · checked 2026-08-23
