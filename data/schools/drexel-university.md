## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (College of Arts and Sciences) offers both a BA and a BS in Mathematics, with a distinctive computing-course sequence. Accelerated BA-Mathematics/MS-Biostatistics 5-year option available.

Source: [Drexel University Catalog](https://catalog.drexel.edu/undergraduate/collegeofartsandsciences/mathematicsbs-ms/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Minor in Theatre (Performance or Production tracks) via the Antoinette Westphal College of Media Arts & Design; a broader Minor in Performing Arts is also available. No standalone Theatre major was confirmed.

Source: [Drexel University Catalog](https://catalog.drexel.edu/undergraduate/collegeofmediaartsanddesign/theatre/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Drexel Theatre Company (Co-Op Theatre Company) produces 3 Mainstage shows/year at Mandell Theater and the URBN Center Black Box. Drexel Players is a long-running student club (founded 1963); Drexel Performing Arts Ensembles are open to all students.

Source: [Drexel University Performing Arts](https://drexel.edu/performingarts/theatre/drexel-theatre-company) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Drexel Performing Arts Ensembles are 'open to all Drexel University students regardless of college or major,' with a mix of audition and non-audition ensembles; students from any major may audition for Mainstage shows and performance scholarships.

Source: [Drexel University Performing Arts](https://drexel.edu/performingarts/theatre/drexel-theatre-company/mainstage-audition-information) · checked 2026-08-23
