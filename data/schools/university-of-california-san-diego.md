## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers seven BS tracks: Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Mathematics-Computer Science, Mathematics-Economics, Mathematics-Applied Science, Probability & Statistics, and Mathematical Biology. Its single BA track is specifically Mathematics-Secondary Education.

Source: [UC San Diego Department of Mathematics](https://math.ucsd.edu/students/undergraduate/majors) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theatre and Dance offers a major and minor in Theatre, a major and minor in Dance, and a double major combining both. Any admitted student may declare — no audition required to declare.

Source: [UC San Diego Department of Theatre and Dance](https://theatre.ucsd.edu/admissions/undergraduate/theatre-minor.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

More than a dozen productions premiere each academic year, ranging from student-directed black-box shows to full-scale faculty-directed mainstage productions.

Source: [UC San Diego Department of Theatre and Dance](https://theatre.ucsd.edu/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

No audition required to declare the major/minor. To audition for department productions generally, students must have completed or be enrolled in TDAC 1 (Introduction to Acting) — a low bar non-majors can meet. A separate spring concert has its own audition process.

Source: [UC San Diego Department of Theatre and Dance](https://theatre.ucsd.edu/admissions/undergraduate/auditioning.html) · checked 2026-08-23
