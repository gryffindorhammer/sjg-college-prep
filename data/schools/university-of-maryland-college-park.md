## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (College of Computer, Mathematical & Natural Sciences) offers a B.S. in Mathematics with four tracks: traditional, applied mathematics, secondary education, and statistics. Core sequence is MATH 140/141/240/241 (or the honors 340/341 sequence); at least four major courses must be taken at College Park.

Source: [UMD Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate](https://www-math.umd.edu/undergraduate.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor (not limited-enrollment)

School of Theatre, Dance, and Performance Studies (TDPS) offers a B.A. in Theatre (55 credits, 28 at the 300/400 level) with faculty expertise including musical theatre; housed in the Clarice Smith Performing Arts Center. Not a limited-enrollment program — any UMD student may declare it as a major or double major, and no audition/review/interview is required to declare.

Source: [TDPS — Bachelor of Arts in Theatre](https://tdps.umd.edu/academic-programs/ba-theatre) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

TDPS presents 4-5 mainstage productions a year plus student-run dance and theatre performance series. Student organizations include the Kreativity Diversity Troupe (performance group centering diversity) and Sketchup (student sketch-comedy troupe).

Source: [TDPS — Student Organizations](https://tdps.umd.edu/student-organizations) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions for TDPS productions and student performance groups are open to all UMD students regardless of major, and students can also take TDPS classes without declaring the major.

Source: [TDPS — FAQs](https://tdps.umd.edu/resources-facilities/faqs) · checked 2026-08-23
