Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics offers a liberal-arts major requiring a minimum of 9 full courses (or equivalent), spanning introductory through applied math, statistics, and operations research; C-/P minimum in all courses counted toward the major.Oberlin Department of Mathematics — Handbook for Majors · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor — Dept. of Theater season includes three faculty/guest-directed mainstage productions in Wurtzel Theater plus several student-directed productions in Kander Theater; contemporary musicals (e.g. Urinetown, Cabaret) staged roughly every other year. Distinct from the Conservatory's separate, audition-admitted Musical Theater major.Oberlin Department of Theater — Season · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsOberlin Student Theater Association (OSTA) is fully student-run, producing five fully-staged shows a year plus playwriting workshops, staged readings, and a twice-yearly 24-hour theater competition. Oberlin Musical Theater Association (OMTA) coordinates, finances, and produces student-run alternative musical theater and workshops.The Oberlin Theater Club · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationCasting for department productions is open to all Oberlin students regardless of year or major — the same is true of OSTA and OMTA's student-run shows, making it easy for non-majors to perform each semester.Oberlin Department of Theater — Curriculum · checked 2026-08-23
