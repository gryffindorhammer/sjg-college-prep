Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics (College of Computer, Mathematical & Natural Sciences) offers a B.S. in Mathematics with four tracks: traditional, applied mathematics, secondary education, and statistics. Core sequence is MATH 140/141/240/241 (or the honors 340/341 sequence); at least four major courses must be taken at College Park.UMD Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor and minor (not limited-enrollment) — School of Theatre, Dance, and Performance Studies (TDPS) offers a B.A. in Theatre (55 credits, 28 at the 300/400 level) with faculty expertise including musical theatre; housed in the Clarice Smith Performing Arts Center. Not a limited-enrollment program — any UMD student may declare it as a major or double major, and no audition/review/interview is required to declare.TDPS — Bachelor of Arts in Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsTDPS presents 4-5 mainstage productions a year plus student-run dance and theatre performance series. Student organizations include the Kreativity Diversity Troupe (performance group centering diversity) and Sketchup (student sketch-comedy troupe).TDPS — Student Organizations · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationAuditions for TDPS productions and student performance groups are open to all UMD students regardless of major, and students can also take TDPS classes without declaring the major.TDPS — FAQs · checked 2026-08-23
