## Mathematics major

BA (~54-55 credits) and BS (~65 credits) in Mathematics; related BS options in Applied Mathematics, Mathematics & Physics, Mathematics & Computer Science, plus accelerated BA/BS-to-MA/MS pathways.

Source: [Temple University Bulletin](https://bulletin.temple.edu/undergraduate/science-technology/mathematics-bs/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Minor in Theater (20 credits, up to 6 transfer credits), open to students in other departments/colleges. Separate Theater BA and Musical Theater BFA majors are audition-based.

Source: [Temple University Bulletin](https://bulletin.temple.edu/undergraduate/theater-film-media-arts/theater-minor/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Temple Theaters stages a full season yearly serving 300+ Theater majors and ~20 MFA candidates. Student orgs include the Musical Theater Cooperative and Alpha Psi Omega (theater honor fraternity).

Source: [Temple University (TFMA academics pages)](https://www.temple.edu/academics/schools-and-colleges/school-of-theater-film-and-media-arts/tfma-ug-grad-theater-productions-facilities) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Exact productions-per-year count not confirmed.

## Non-major participation

Mixed evidence: the minor is explicitly open to any student, but mainstage performance courses are described as available to Acting-concentration students 'and to other students in the department who meet prerequisite requirements' — ambiguous on general non-major mainstage auditions.
