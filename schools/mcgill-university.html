Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics and Statistics offers a Major Mathematics B.Sc. (54 credits), Honours Mathematics (60-63 credits), a Major/Honours Statistics track (with A.Stat professional-accreditation eligibility), and a combined Mathematics & Computer Science major.McGill Mathematics and Statistics — Major Mathematics (B.Sc.) · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor and minor concentration (English Dept.; liberal-arts, not conservatory) — Dept. of English offers a Drama and Theatre major/minor concentration (part of the B.A. in English) that studies drama and theatre broadly as a liberal-arts discipline rather than providing professional acting training; courses include Introduction to Performance, Directing for the Theatre, and theatre history. Productions are staged at Moyse Hall.McGill Undergraduate Admissions — English: Drama and Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsPlayers' Theatre (SSMU-run, founded 1921) and Tuesday Night Cafe Theatre (student-run, affiliated with the English Dept.) both stage regular seasons directed and performed by McGill students, alongside the Moyse Hall departmental season and other groups like the Arts Undergraduate Theatre Society.Tuesday Night Cafe Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
