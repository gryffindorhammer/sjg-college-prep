## Mathematics major

The Mathematics Department offers a BA and BS in Mathematics, with flexibility for pure, applied, and interdisciplinary study; undergraduate research and honors options are available.

Source: [Penn Mathematics](https://www.math.upenn.edu/undergraduate) · checked 2026-09-08

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Theatre Arts offers an interdisciplinary BA in Theatre Arts and a minor, combining performance, directing, design, dramaturgy, and scholarship within the College of Arts and Sciences.

Source: [Penn Theatre Arts](https://theatrearts.sas.upenn.edu/) · checked 2026-09-08

## Student productions & clubs

Penn has a large student-performing-arts ecosystem with student-run theater, musical-theater, comedy, a cappella, dance, and music organizations; the Platt Student Performing Arts House supports many of them.

Source: [Platt Student Performing Arts House](https://plattstudentperformingarts.com/) · checked 2026-09-08

## Non-major participation

Student performing-arts groups include opportunities for performers and production teams, and Theatre Arts courses and activities are available beyond the major.

Source: [Penn Theatre Arts](https://theatrearts.sas.upenn.edu/) · checked 2026-09-08
