Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorEberly College of Science offers a B.S. in Mathematics with six options (actuarial science, applied/industrial, computational mathematics, graduate-study track, systems analysis) plus a B.A. track; entrance to the major requires MATH 140 completed with a C or better and a 2.00 cumulative GPA.Penn State Eberly College of Science — Mathematics Majors · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor (BA liberal-arts track; separately-admitted, audition-based BFA in Musical Theatre) — School of Theatre offers a liberal-arts B.A. in Theatre (pairs well with a double major/minors) alongside a highly selective, prescreen/audition-based B.F.A. in Musical Theatre — the largest dedicated musical-theatre faculty in the country, with alumni regularly working on Broadway.Penn State School of Theatre — BFA Musical Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsPenn State Centre Stage and OffStage seasons offer roles as assistant directors, dramaturgs, and crew; numerous student theatre groups also direct, design, and perform independently — including No Refund Theatre, a student-run, all-free organization founded in 1993 that stages a mix of established works and original student-written plays/musicals each semester.No Refund Theatre · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationNo Refund Theatre is explicitly open to all students "regardless of major" to perform, direct, and produce; the BA Theatre program is a liberal-arts major (no audition to declare) that many students pair with a different primary major or minor. The BFA Musical Theatre track, by contrast, is a separately-admitted, prescreen/audition-required program.No Refund Theatre — How to Get Involved · checked 2026-08-23
