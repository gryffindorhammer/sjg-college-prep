## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers the major paired with a BA or BS; six 'named options' combine math with an applied focus area. BA requires 7 distinct courses (~21 credits) spanning at least two areas of advanced mathematics.

Source: [UW-Madison Department of Mathematics / Guide](https://www.math.wisc.edu/undergraduate/programs/major-options/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: UW-Madison math is generally well-regarded (historically top-tier in U.S. News grad math rankings), but a current sourced figure wasn't confirmed this session.

## Theater program

Type: major and certificate

Dept. of Theatre and Drama offers a BS in Theatre and Drama (declared directly upon admission) AND a standalone Certificate in Theatre explicitly for 'any UW-Madison undergraduate student who is NOT a declared Theatre major' (16+ credits, 2.5 GPA in certificate coursework), flexible toward acting, technical theatre, costume design, or stage management.

Source: [UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama / Guide](https://theatre.wisc.edu/academics/theatre-certificate/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

University Theatre is UW-Madison's main student-facing producing organization within the department.

Source: [UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama](https://theatre.wisc.edu/productions-and-tickets/student-opportunities/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Annual production count not confirmed.

## Non-major participation

Many Theatre and Drama courses are explicitly open to non-majors. University Theatre auditions are open to 'all current and incoming UW-Madison students, regardless of major.' The Theatre Certificate is a formal, credit-bearing path built specifically for non-majors.

Source: [UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama](https://theatre.wisc.edu/academics/) · checked 2026-08-23
