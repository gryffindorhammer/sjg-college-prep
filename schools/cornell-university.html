Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics (College of Arts & Sciences) awards a B.A.; students may be admitted to the major after completing a semester each of multivariable calculus and linear algebra plus a programming course (C- or better). Seven concentration options span analysis, algebra, topology, geometry, probability, statistics, and logic; all major courses require a letter grade with a C- minimum.Cornell Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate Major · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMinor (no standalone theater major; four related minors) — Dept. of Performing and Media Arts (PMA) — students from any major can pursue a Theatre minor, alongside separate Dance, Film, and Performing & Media Arts minors. PMA productions run out of the Schwartz Center for the Performing Arts (four theatres, two acting classrooms, dance and ballet studios).Cornell Performing and Media Arts — Undergraduate · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsPMA stages dozens of professional and student-run productions a year at the Schwartz Center involving hundreds of student performers and crew; independent student-run musical theater clubs include Melodramatics.Cornell PMA — News/Auditions · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationAuditions for Schwartz Center productions are open to all Cornell students, faculty, and staff, not just PMA majors/minors.Cornell PMA — Frequently Asked Questions · checked 2026-08-23
