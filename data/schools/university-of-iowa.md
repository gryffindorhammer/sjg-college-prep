## Mathematics major

BA and BS in Mathematics: Program A (business/government/grad-school prep) and Program B (secondary teaching licensure); a combined major with another department is also available.

Source: [University of Iowa Dept. of Mathematics](https://math.uiowa.edu/undergraduate/ba-mathematics) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Minor in Theatre Arts (15 semester hours, 12+ at 2000-level, 2.0 GPA). A Theatre Arts BA major is also offered with no audition required to declare.

Source: [University of Iowa Dept. of Theatre Arts](https://theatre.uiowa.edu/minor-theatre-arts) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

~20-25 public productions staged per year by the Department of Theatre Arts.

Source: [University of Iowa Dept. of Theatre Arts](https://theatre.uiowa.edu/production) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Independent student theater club names not confirmed.

## Non-major participation

'Majors, non-majors, and community members alike can get involved in production.' No audition required to major/minor; general auditions (priority to majors, then other UI students, then community) held at the start of fall plus November and March.

Source: [University of Iowa Dept. of Theatre Arts](https://theatre.uiowa.edu/undergraduate) · checked 2026-08-23
