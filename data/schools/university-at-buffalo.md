## Mathematics major

BA and BS in Mathematics, Dept. of Mathematics. BA has a Computing and Applied Mathematics concentration; BS has a General Study in Mathematics concentration. Requires 2.5 GPA in Calc I/II/III to continue.

Source: [UB Department of Mathematics](https://www.buffalo.edu/cas/math.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Minor in Theatre and a separate Minor in Dance, Dept. of Theatre and Dance, covering acting, directing, dramaturgy, stage management, and design/tech.

Source: [UB Dept. of Theatre and Dance](https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/theatre-dance/undergraduate/minor-theatre.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Department season typically includes 2 musicals, 2 plays, and 4 dance concerts/year, plus student-directed projects.

Source: [UB Dept. of Theatre and Dance](https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/theatre-dance/about/theatre-dance-ub.html) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Independent student theater club names not confirmed.

## Non-major participation

Auditions open to all UB undergrads (except Zodiaque Dance Company) with one semester at UB and good standing; musicals/plays are credit-bearing. Non-majors can also take on design/production roles.

Source: [UB Dept. of Theatre and Dance](https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/theatre-dance/auditions-interviews.html) · checked 2026-08-23
