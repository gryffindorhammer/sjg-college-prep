## Mathematics major

BA in Mathematics through the Department of Mathematics; Honors Analysis track available. Joint BA/MS pathway and a separate Computational and Applied Mathematics (CAAM) program also offered.

Source: [UChicago Department of Mathematics / College Catalog](https://mathematics.uchicago.edu/undergraduate/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Theater and Performance Studies (TAPS) at the Logan Center for the Arts. Major requires 11 electives (8+ TAPS-numbered) plus a capstone BA project across acting, directing, playwriting, design, dramaturgy, or performance-studies tracks. Over 800 students/year take TAPS courses even though far fewer major/minor.

Source: [UChicago Academic Catalog](http://collegecatalog.uchicago.edu/thecollege/theaterperformancestudies/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

University Theater (UT), the umbrella student-run org, puts on 35+ shows/year across 5 spaces, including the Short Play Showcase, Theater[24] (quarterly 24-hour new-work festival), The Dean's Men (student Shakespeare company), and Off-Off Campus (improv troupe).

Source: [University Theater (UT) at UChicago](https://ut.uchicago.edu/about/ut) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

UT membership is explicitly 'open to any and all majors' and 'all interested UChicago students, regardless of divisional or departmental affiliation' — no TAPS major/minor required to act, design, direct, or manage.

Source: [University Theater (UT) at UChicago](https://ut.uchicago.edu/about/ut) · checked 2026-08-23
