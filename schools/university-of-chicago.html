Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorBA in Mathematics through the Department of Mathematics; Honors Analysis track available. Joint BA/MS pathway and a separate Computational and Applied Mathematics (CAAM) program also offered.UChicago Department of Mathematics / College Catalog · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor and minor — Theater and Performance Studies (TAPS) at the Logan Center for the Arts. Major requires 11 electives (8+ TAPS-numbered) plus a capstone BA project across acting, directing, playwriting, design, dramaturgy, or performance-studies tracks. Over 800 students/year take TAPS courses even though far fewer major/minor.UChicago Academic Catalog · checked 2026-08-23
