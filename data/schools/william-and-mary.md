## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (College of Arts & Sciences) requires proficiency in calculus I & II, linear algebra, multivariable calculus, and foundations of mathematics, plus Abstract Algebra and/or Elementary Analysis; four concentrations available (including Applied Mathematics and Standard Mathematics), totaling roughly 7-8 advanced courses.

Source: [William & Mary Mathematics — Undergraduate Program](https://www.wm.edu/as/mathematics/undergrad/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theatre, Speech, and Dance (College of Arts & Sciences) — no audition required to major or minor; department productions are cast from open auditions each semester in keeping with the department's liberal-arts philosophy.

Source: [William & Mary Theatre & Performance](https://www.wm.edu/as/theatre-performance/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Department mounts a full production season across theatre and dance; student organizations include the student-run Sinfonicron Light Opera Company, which produces an annual musical/light opera.

Source: [William & Mary — Production Opportunities](https://www.wm.edu/as/theatre-performance/theatre/production_opportunities/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions are open to freshmen through seniors, majors and non-majors alike; non-majors audition with a provided cold reading (or an optional prepared piece), and musical-theatre auditions add a song with an accompanist provided. Participation on- and off-stage (design, shops, running crew) is open to students from every major on campus.

Source: [William & Mary — Production Opportunities](https://www.wm.edu/as/tsd/theatre/production_opportunities/) · checked 2026-08-23
