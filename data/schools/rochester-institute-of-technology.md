## Mathematics major

School of Mathematics and Statistics (College of Science) offers a B.S. in Applied Mathematics focused on real-world problem solving (GPS, manufacturing, encryption, etc.); College of Science requires Experiential Learning before graduation, fulfillable via co-op, undergrad research, or designated EL courses.

Source: [RIT — Applied Mathematics BS](https://www.rit.edu/science/study/applied-mathematics-bs) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor (no theater major)

School of Performing Arts (College of Liberal Arts) offers a Theatre Arts Minor — open to both hearing and deaf/hard-of-hearing students — covering theory and practice; RIT has no standalone theater/musical-theater major.

Source: [RIT — Theatre Arts Minor](https://www.rit.edu/study/theatre-arts-minor) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Joint theatrical/dance seasons are staged by the College of Liberal Arts Theatre Arts Program together with NTID's Performing Arts Program (est. 1974), spanning comedies, dramas, musicals, and classics; NTID Performing Arts also offers a certificate documenting participation in performance or technical-theatre activities.

Source: [RIT/NTID and School of Performing Arts — 2024-2025 Season](https://www.rit.edu/news/rit-ntid-and-school-performing-arts-present-2024-2025-theatrical-and-dance-season) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions and casting are open to all RIT students, staff, and faculty regardless of major; productions are explicitly open to students of any background campus-wide.

Source: [RIT — Theatre Auditions and Casting](https://www.rit.edu/performingarts/theatre-auditions-and-casting) · checked 2026-08-23
