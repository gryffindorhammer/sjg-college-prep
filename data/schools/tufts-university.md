## Mathematics major

BA/BS in Mathematics, plus BA/BS tracks in Applied Mathematics and Pure Mathematics (10 courses beyond Calc II); 5th-year BA/MS option available.

Source: [Tufts Department of Mathematics](https://math.tufts.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/babs-mathematics) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Department of Theatre, Dance, and Performance Studies (TDPS) offers a major and minor in Theatre and Performance Studies (minor: 5 courses), plus a separate Dance minor.

Source: [Tufts TDPS](https://tdps.tufts.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/minor-theatre-and-performance-studies) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Dept. produces 3 faculty-directed mainstage shows/year plus 2 dance concerts. Independent student groups: Torn Ticket II (student-run musical theatre) and 3Ps (Pen, Paint & Pretzels, mostly straight plays), each staging a production most semesters.

Source: [Tufts TDPS; Torn Ticket II](https://tdps.tufts.edu/about) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

'You do not have to be a TDPS major or minor to audition for department shows.' Torn Ticket II and 3Ps are both open to all students.

Source: [Tufts TDPS student organizations page](https://tdps.tufts.edu/resources/student-organizations) · checked 2026-08-23
