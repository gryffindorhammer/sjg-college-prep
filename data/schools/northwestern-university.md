## Mathematics major

The Department of Mathematics offers a BA in Mathematics and a BS in Mathematics, with options spanning pure mathematics, applied mathematics, probability/statistics, and mathematical methods in the social sciences.

Source: [Northwestern Mathematics — Undergraduate Program](https://math.northwestern.edu/undergraduate/) · checked 2026-08-24

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Northwestern's School of Communication offers undergraduate majors and minors in Theatre, with coursework and production opportunities across performance, directing, design, playwriting, and theatre history.

Source: [Northwestern School of Communication — Theatre](https://communication.northwestern.edu/academics/theatre/) · checked 2026-08-24

## Student productions & clubs

Student theatre is unusually active, including the student-run Waa-Mu Show and many productions through the Virginia Wadsworth Wirtz Center for the Performing Arts and student groups.

Source: [Northwestern Wirtz Center](https://wirtz.northwestern.edu/) · checked 2026-08-24

## Non-major participation

Northwestern students can take theatre courses and participate in many productions and student organizations regardless of major; individual auditions and course prerequisites vary by opportunity.

Source: [Northwestern School of Communication — Theatre](https://communication.northwestern.edu/academics/theatre/) · checked 2026-08-24
