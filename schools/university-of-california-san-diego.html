Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics offers seven BS tracks: Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Mathematics-Computer Science, Mathematics-Economics, Mathematics-Applied Science, Probability & Statistics, and Mathematical Biology. Its single BA track is specifically Mathematics-Secondary Education.UC San Diego Department of Mathematics · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor and minor — Dept. of Theatre and Dance offers a major and minor in Theatre, a major and minor in Dance, and a double major combining both. Any admitted student may declare — no audition required to declare.UC San Diego Department of Theatre and Dance · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsMore than a dozen productions premiere each academic year, ranging from student-directed black-box shows to full-scale faculty-directed mainstage productions.UC San Diego Department of Theatre and Dance · checked 2026-08-23

To audition for department productions generally, students must have completed or be enrolled in TDAC 1 (Introduction to Acting) — a low bar non-majors can meet.
