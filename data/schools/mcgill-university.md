## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics offers a Major Mathematics B.Sc. (54 credits), Honours Mathematics (60-63 credits), a Major/Honours Statistics track (with A.Stat professional-accreditation eligibility), and a combined Mathematics & Computer Science major.

Source: [McGill Mathematics and Statistics — Major Mathematics (B.Sc.)](https://www.mcgill.ca/mathstat/undergraduate/programs/b-sc/major-mathematics-b-sc) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor concentration (English Dept.; liberal-arts, not conservatory)

Dept. of English offers a Drama and Theatre major/minor concentration (part of the B.A. in English) that studies drama and theatre broadly as a liberal-arts discipline rather than providing professional acting training; courses include Introduction to Performance, Directing for the Theatre, and theatre history. Productions are staged at Moyse Hall.

Source: [McGill Undergraduate Admissions — English: Drama and Theatre](https://www.mcgill.ca/undergraduate-admissions/program/english-drama-and-theatre) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Players' Theatre (SSMU-run, founded 1921) and Tuesday Night Cafe Theatre (student-run, affiliated with the English Dept.) both stage regular seasons directed and performed by McGill students, alongside the Moyse Hall departmental season and other groups like the Arts Undergraduate Theatre Society.

Source: [Tuesday Night Cafe Theatre](https://tuesdaynightcafetheatre.wordpress.com/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Players' Theatre explicitly welcomes beginners and anyone wanting to act, learn, or crew — "beginners are always welcome" — regardless of major; it functions as a general campus resource rather than a departmental program.

Source: [Discovering Theatre at McGill: Where and How to Get Involved](https://www.hercampus.com/school/mcgill/discovering-theatre-mcgill-where-and-how-get-involved/) · checked 2026-08-23
