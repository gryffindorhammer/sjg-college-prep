## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers an A.B. in Mathematics, a B.S. in Mathematics (more electives at 300+ level), and an A.B. joint major in Mathematics and Economics.

Source: [Lafayette College Department of Mathematics](https://math.lafayette.edu/degree-requirements/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Dept. of Theater offers both a major and a Minor in Theater (5 credits minimum: Introduction to Theater, two practicums, plus 3 additional designated courses).

Source: [Lafayette College Department of Theater](https://lafayette.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/catalog/majors/theater) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Faculty-directed mainstage season each year (2026-27 includes an outdoor Poe-inspired adaptation, a musical, a mystery, a senior capstone production, and the return of the Fringe Festival) plus separate student-produced shows.

Source: [Lafayette College Theater Department / Lafayette Today](https://theater.lafayette.edu/2025-2026-season/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Exact total production count/year not stated.

## Non-major participation

'All roles are open to all students, regardless of major or class year,' and the College Theater 'welcomes all matriculating students, regardless of major or minor, to participate in productions.'

Source: [Lafayette College Theater Department / Lafayette Today](https://today.lafayette.edu/2026/08/13/get-involved-in-theater-at-lafayette/) · checked 2026-08-23
