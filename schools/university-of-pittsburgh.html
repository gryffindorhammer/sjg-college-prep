Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics (Dietrich School of Arts & Sciences) offers a B.S. in Mathematics, plus B.S. tracks in Mathematics-Economics and Applied Mathematics; after the calculus sequence and three basic theoretical courses, students complete six or more upper-level courses (C or better in each, no course double-counting). Majors declared Fall 2018 or later must also complete MATH 0500 Professional Development.Pitt Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate Degree Programs · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMajor, minor, and certificate (21-credit Musical Theatre Certificate open to all students) — Dept. of Theatre Arts offers a Theatre Arts major and minor, plus a 21-credit Musical Theatre Certificate open to all students regardless of major. No audition or portfolio is required to join the program or enroll in classes.Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Musical Theatre Certificate · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsDepartment stages 3-5 mainstage shows a year (faculty/guest-directed) plus 3-12 lab shows directed by graduate or advanced undergraduate students.Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Current Season · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationAuditions for all productions, including musicals, are open to majors and non-majors alike — first-years are explicitly invited to audition, and stages are open to students from any school or major across the university; non-majors can also apply as designers, dramaturgs, backstage crew, or assistant directors.Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Undergraduate · checked 2026-08-23
