## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a B.A., a B.S. in Mathematics, and a B.S. in Applied Mathematics, plus a minor, actuarial-science prep, and math-teacher certification tracks.

Source: [SUNY Geneseo Department of Mathematics](https://www.geneseo.edu/math/programs/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

No Theatre major is offered — only minors, via the Department for the Performing Arts: Theatre minor (24 credit hours), Musical Theatre minor, Dance minor (audition-based at the 200-level), and Dance History minor.

Source: [SUNY Geneseo Department for the Performing Arts](https://www.geneseo.edu/theatre_dance/theatre_minor) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

The department's Mainstage Series presents 5 productions/year across Theatre, Musical Theatre, and Dance. The student-run Musical Theatre Club co-sponsors the fall mainstage musical and produces a revue each semester.

Source: [The Lamron (student newspaper); SUNY Geneseo Performing Arts](https://www.thelamron.com/knights-life/geneseo-performing-arts-announces-2026-27-season) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

The department advertises 'Open Auditions for All Students' each season; since Theatre is minor-only (no major), most participants are inherently non-majors by structure.

Source: [SUNY Geneseo Events Calendar](https://events.geneseo.edu/event/department_of_theatre_and_dance_open_auditions_for_all_students) · checked 2026-08-23
