Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics offers a B.A., B.S., Honors B.S., B.S. in Applied Mathematics, and a joint B.A. in Mathematics & Statistics. All tracks require the "160" calculus sequence and MATH 235 (Linear Algebra) as a foundation before further major coursework.University of Rochester Department of Mathematics · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMinor — International Theatre Program (partnered with the Dept. of English) offers a Minor in Theater, minimum 24 credits, covering production, performance, and dramatic literature.University of Rochester International Theatre Program · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsITP produces four major productions a year, directed/designed by professional guest artists, plus a student-run One-Act New Play Festival most spring semesters. Student groups include Off Broadway On Campus and the ROC Players; the Music Dept. occasionally offers a Musical Theatre Workshop.International Theatre Program — Productions · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationAuditions are open to all University of Rochester students regardless of major, including first-years, Take 5 scholars, and graduate students — no prior experience necessary. Cast members, assistant directors, and stage managers earn a minimum of four academic (English) credits per production.ITP Get Involved/Audition FAQ · checked 2026-08-23
