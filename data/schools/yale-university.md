## Mathematics major

BA in Mathematics: 10 courses (2250+), incl. multivariable analysis/calc plus analysis or complex analysis, plus a senior seminar/essay. BS adds 2 advanced physical-science courses. Intensive-major and combined-degree (CS, Econ, Philosophy, Physics, BS/MS) options exist.

Source: [Yale Department of Mathematics](https://math.yale.edu/undergraduates/the-major/math-intensive-math) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major

Undergraduate major: Theater, Dance, and Performance Studies (TDPS) — performance history/theory plus practice (acting, directing, playwriting, design, dramaturgy, dance, musical theater). Majors may cross-register into David Geffen School of Drama (graduate-only conservatory) courses with permission.

Source: [Yale TDPS](https://tdps.yale.edu/welcome) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

100+ undergraduate productions/year campuswide via the Yale Drama Coalition (YDC). The Yale Dramatic Association ('the Dramat'), the largest undergrad theater org, produces 6 shows/year: 2 student-run Experimental Shows, the first-year 'FroShow,' Fall and Spring Mainstages, and an annual Commencement Musical.

Source: [Yale College Arts](https://collegearts.yale.edu/organizations/yale-drama-coalition/performance-groups/yale-dramatic-association) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Explicitly open to non-majors: 'any undergraduate student — whether majoring in an arts program or not — can apply for support for a project' through YDC/Dramat; auditions and crew calls go out to the whole undergraduate population.

Source: [Yale TDPS Applicant FAQ](https://tdps.yale.edu/applicant-faq) · checked 2026-08-23
