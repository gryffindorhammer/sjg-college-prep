## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (Faculty of Arts & Science) offers a Mathematics Major, a Mathematics Minor, and several Specialist programs (Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Mathematics & Physics, Mathematics & Philosophy, Mathematical Applications in Economics & Finance, Mathematics & Its Applications). Leads to an Honours Bachelor of Science.

Source: [U of T Faculty of Arts & Science Academic Calendar](https://artsci.calendar.utoronto.ca/section/Mathematics) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major

Centre for Drama, Theatre & Performance Studies (CDTPS) offers Drama Specialist, Major, and Minor programs combining scholarship with practical training.

Source: [Centre for Drama, Theatre & Performance Studies](https://www.cdtps.utoronto.ca/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

UC Follies, University College's student-run theatre troupe (active since 1880), stages a season of multiple productions/year. Hart House Theatre hosts the annual U of T Drama Festival (since 1936) plus its own Mainstage season.

Source: [UC Follies; Hart House Theatre](https://ucfolliestheatre.wordpress.com/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Exact production count not confirmed.

## Non-major participation

UC Follies describes itself as 'an inclusive space for amateur and professionally-minded theatre makers,' open regardless of major. The Hart House U of T Drama Festival is explicitly open to 'U of T students from all campuses,' not restricted to Drama program students.

Source: [UC Follies; Hart House Theatre](https://harthouse.ca/theatre/show/2026-hart-house-u-of-t-drama-festival) · checked 2026-08-23
