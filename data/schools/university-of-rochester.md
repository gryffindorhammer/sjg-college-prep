## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a B.A., B.S., Honors B.S., B.S. in Applied Mathematics, and a joint B.A. in Mathematics & Statistics. All tracks require the "160" calculus sequence and MATH 235 (Linear Algebra) as a foundation before further major coursework.

Source: [University of Rochester Department of Mathematics](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/undergraduate/index.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

International Theatre Program (partnered with the Dept. of English) offers a Minor in Theater, minimum 24 credits, covering production, performance, and dramatic literature.

Source: [University of Rochester International Theatre Program](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/theatre/academics/minor.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

ITP produces four major productions a year, directed/designed by professional guest artists, plus a student-run One-Act New Play Festival most spring semesters. Student groups include Off Broadway On Campus and the ROC Players; the Music Dept. occasionally offers a Musical Theatre Workshop.

Source: [International Theatre Program — Productions](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/theatre/productions/index.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions are open to all University of Rochester students regardless of major, including first-years, Take 5 scholars, and graduate students — no prior experience necessary. Cast members, assistant directors, and stage managers earn a minimum of four academic (English) credits per production.

Source: [ITP Get Involved/Audition FAQ](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/theatre/auditions/faq.html) · checked 2026-08-23
