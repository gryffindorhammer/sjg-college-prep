## Mathematics major

Mathematics offered as a Bachelor of Arts (more flexible/interdisciplinary) or Bachelor of Science (more focused); upper-division study can target math grad school, computational/applied math, or secondary math teaching.

Source: [UC Davis Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/undergrad/orientation/prosp_majors) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theatre and Dance offers one undergraduate major in Dramatic Art (focus in Theatre or Dance) and a Theatre and Dance minor; 120+ undergrads major in Dramatic Art.

Source: [UC Davis Department of Theatre and Dance](https://arts.ucdavis.edu/general-information/theatre-and-dance-major) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Five dedicated performance spaces. Notable student-driven series: 'Written and Directed By' (undergraduate-written one-act plays) and the '180 Series' of student production courses.

Source: [UC Davis Department of Theatre and Dance](https://arts.ucdavis.edu/theatre-dance-current-season) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Exact mainstage productions/year not stated.

## Non-major participation

The '180 Series' explicitly welcomes 'interested non-affiliated students' (non-majors) into practical production work; auditions for productions like 'Written and Directed By' are open to all UC Davis students, not just majors.

Source: [UC Davis Department of Theatre and Dance](https://ucdtd180.weebly.com/) · checked 2026-08-23
