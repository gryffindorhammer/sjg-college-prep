## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (College of Arts & Sciences) awards a B.A.; students may be admitted to the major after completing a semester each of multivariable calculus and linear algebra plus a programming course (C- or better). Seven concentration options span analysis, algebra, topology, geometry, probability, statistics, and logic; all major courses require a letter grade with a C- minimum.

Source: [Cornell Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate Major](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/m/Undergraduate/Major/major) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor (no standalone theater major; four related minors)

Dept. of Performing and Media Arts (PMA) — students from any major can pursue a Theatre minor, alongside separate Dance, Film, and Performing & Media Arts minors. PMA productions run out of the Schwartz Center for the Performing Arts (four theatres, two acting classrooms, dance and ballet studios).

Source: [Cornell Performing and Media Arts — Undergraduate](https://pma.cornell.edu/undergraduate-major) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

PMA stages dozens of professional and student-run productions a year at the Schwartz Center involving hundreds of student performers and crew; independent student-run musical theater clubs include Melodramatics.

Source: [Cornell PMA — News/Auditions](https://pma.cornell.edu/news/auditions-november-14-spring-productions) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions for Schwartz Center productions are open to all Cornell students, faculty, and staff, not just PMA majors/minors.

Source: [Cornell PMA — Frequently Asked Questions](https://pma.cornell.edu/frequently-asked-questions) · checked 2026-08-23
