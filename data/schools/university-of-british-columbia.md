## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a BSc in Mathematics with concentrations in Applied Mathematics, Pure Mathematics, or Statistics, plus a Combined Major in Computer Science and Mathematics and Combined Honours with another science; entry by transfer to year 2 requires 6 credits of first-year calculus and a computing requirement (CPSC 110, or CPSC 103+107, or MATH 210).

Source: [UBC Mathematics (BSc)](https://you.ubc.ca/ubc_programs/mathematics-vancouver-bsc/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: BA and BFA (no dedicated musical theatre track)

Dept. of Theatre and Film offers a BA in Theatre and Film (liberal arts) and a BFA with tracks in Acting, Design & Production, and Film Production; the BFA Acting program combines professional training with theatre studies and a liberal-arts grounding. No dedicated musical-theatre BFA was found.

Source: [UBC Theatre and Film — Undergraduate Programs](https://theatrefilm.ubc.ca/undergraduate/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

UBC Musical Theatre Troupe (student club) produces two full-length musicals a year; UBC Players Club, founded 1915, is the university's oldest club and stages performance art of all kinds.

Source: [UBC Student Services — Club Spotlight: UBC Musical Theatre Troupe](https://students.ubc.ca/ubclife/club-spotlight-ubc-musical-theatre-troupe) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

UBC Musical Theatre Troupe casting is open to all UBC students regardless of major, welcoming all experience and skill levels; UBC Players Club has likewise connected students, faculty, and community members through performance for over a century.

Source: [UBC Musical Theatre Troupe — Join the Club](https://ubcmtt.wordpress.com/club-membership/) · checked 2026-08-23
