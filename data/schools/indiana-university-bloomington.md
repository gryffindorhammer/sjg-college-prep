## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a B.A. (flexible, pairs well with a double major/minor) and a B.S. with two tracks: Program I (theory, prepares for math grad school) and Program II (applied). A Math minor is also offered.

Source: [IU Department of Mathematics](https://math.indiana.edu/undergraduate/index.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

Dept. of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance offers an 18-credit-hour Theatre & Drama minor (Intro to Theatre/Script Analysis plus upper-level electives across four concentration areas), designed to combine with majors in other schools. A full Theatre & Drama major and a BFA in Musical Theatre are also offered.

Source: [IU Department of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance](https://theatre.indiana.edu/undergraduate/theatre-drama-ba/theatre-drama-minor.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

University Players (UP), IU's student-run theatre org, reaches roughly 1,300 students/year through directed readings, 10-minute festivals, cabarets, faculty spotlights, audition workshops, and full-length plays/musicals — all productions free. The department also stages a faculty-directed mainstage season.

Source: [IU Department of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance](https://theatre.indiana.edu/undergraduate/student-experience/university-players.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

'Productions are inclusive: majors and non-majors alike participate in acting, design, stage management, and technical roles.' University Players serves 'undergraduate students of all majors and backgrounds.'

Source: [IU Department of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance](https://theatre.indiana.edu/undergraduate/student-experience/university-players.html) · checked 2026-08-23
