## Mathematics major

College of Arts & Sciences Dept. of Mathematics & Statistics offers a BA in Mathematics (Pure or Statistics track) and a joint BA in Mathematics & Mathematics Education.

Source: [Boston University CAS Mathematics & Statistics](https://www.bu.edu/academics/cas/programs/mathematics-statistics/ba/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor (major is an audition-based BFA via the College of Fine Arts)

CFA School of Theatre's flagship major is a BFA in Theatre Arts (Performance); a Theatre Arts Minor is also offered (11-credit theatre core, 7-credit dramatic-literature core, 10 elective credits).

Source: [Boston University CFA School of Theatre](https://www.bu.edu/academics/cfa/programs/school-of-theatre/theatre-arts/minor/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Whether the minor is formally open to students outside CFA was not confirmed.

## Student productions & clubs

Stage Troupe (BU's oldest and largest extracurricular performing-arts group, for undergrads not majoring in theatre) and On Broadway (student musical theater group), alongside the CFA School of Theatre's own season.

Source: [BU Stage Troupe; On Broadway](https://bustagetroupe.squarespace.com/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Stage Troupe auditions are open to all BU undergraduates 'with the exception of Acting/Theatre Arts majors in CFA due to college limitations.' No prior experience, resume, or headshot required.

Source: [BU Stage Troupe (Actors page)](https://bustagetroupe.squarespace.com/actors) · checked 2026-08-23
