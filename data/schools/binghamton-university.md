## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics (Harpur College) offers a BA and BS in Mathematical Sciences, each with mathematics, actuarial science, or data-science/statistics tracks; the BS is more upper-level/proof-oriented and geared toward graduate study. Students must be admitted to Harpur College before declaring the major.

Source: [Binghamton Dept. of Mathematics and Statistics — Undergraduate Program](https://www2.math.binghamton.edu/p/ug) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: BFA major (also offers a minor)

Dept. of Theatre offers a BFA in Musical Theatre — a four-year sequence building acting, singing, and dance skills — alongside a general BA in Theatre and a Theatre Minor (four non-practicum courses plus practicum credits).

Source: [Binghamton Theatre Department — BFA Musical Theatre](https://www.binghamton.edu/theatre/undergraduate/bfa-music-theatre.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Fall Mainstage auditions run the first weekend of school; Spring production auditions run at the end of Fall semester. The department runs Mainstage, Studio, and Open Season productions each year, plus dance classes/productions through the School of the Arts.

Source: [Binghamton Theatre Department — Auditions](https://www.binghamton.edu/theatre/productions/auditions.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Any registered student taking at least 4 credits may audition for any Theatre Department production (Mainstage, Studio, or Open Season) — majors and minors are not required. Dance classes and productions are likewise open to all registered students, and non-majors can also get involved backstage.

Source: [Binghamton Theatre Department — Auditions FAQ](https://www.binghamton.edu/theatre/productions/auditions.html) · checked 2026-08-23
