Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics offers a B.A. (flexible, pairs well with a double major/minor) and a B.S. with two tracks: Program I (theory, prepares for math grad school) and Program II (applied). A Math minor is also offered.IU Department of Mathematics · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programMinor — Dept. of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance offers an 18-credit-hour Theatre & Drama minor (Intro to Theatre/Script Analysis plus upper-level electives across four concentration areas), designed to combine with majors in other schools. A full Theatre & Drama major and a BFA in Musical Theatre are also offered.IU Department of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsUniversity Players (UP), IU's student-run theatre org, reaches roughly 1,300 students/year through directed readings, 10-minute festivals, cabarets, faculty spotlights, audition workshops, and full-length plays/musicals — all productions free. The department also stages a faculty-directed mainstage season.IU Department of Theatre, Drama, and Contemporary Dance · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participation'Productions are inclusive: majors and non-majors alike participate in acting, design, stage management, and technical roles.'
