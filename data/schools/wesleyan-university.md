## Mathematics major

BA in Mathematics, Dept. of Mathematics and Computer Science; course of study planned with a faculty advisor. Honors via thesis or an advanced course sequence including some graduate-level courses.

Source: [Wesleyan Academic Catalog](https://catalog.wesleyan.edu/departments/math/ugrd-math/) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: No math subject ranking found — Wesleyan has no doctoral math program, so it's outside the scope of grad-oriented subject rankings; expected for an undergrad-only LAC department.

## Theater program

Type: major

Theater Department, part of the Center for the Arts. Full major with tracks in playwriting, design (costume/lighting/scenic/media), directing, performance-studies theory, performance art, puppetry, solo performance, technical theater, and community-engaged theater.

Source: [Wesleyan Academic Catalog](https://catalog.wesleyan.edu/departments/thea/ugrd-thea/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Second Stage, the primary student theater org, is student-run and volunteer-staffed, mounting roughly 35 shows/year with about 15 core staff.

Source: [Second Stage (Wesleyan student theater org)](https://www.2ndstage.org/student-eligibility/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

'Theater majors and non-majors pool their talents' — all undergraduates, any class year or major, may apply for Second Stage sponsorship of a show; not all of the ~15 core staff are theater majors.

Source: [Second Stage 'Student Eligibility' page](https://www.2ndstage.org/student-eligibility/) · checked 2026-08-23
