## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a BA only (no separate BS), with two tracks: Pure Mathematics and Applied Mathematics. Rigorous, proof-based curriculum with a strong PhD-preparation pipeline.

Source: [UC Berkeley Department of Mathematics](https://math.berkeley.edu/undergraduate/majoring-mathematics) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major and minor

Dept. of Theater, Dance, and Performance Studies (TDPS) offers a major and minor in Theater and Performance Studies, and separately a major and minor in Dance and Performance Studies. Declaration requires two lower-division courses.

Source: [UC Berkeley TDPS](https://tdps.berkeley.edu/undergrad) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

TDPS mounts Playhouse and Studio productions each year (announced via the department's Callboard); numerous student-run performance groups also exist outside the department.

Source: [UC Berkeley TDPS](https://tdps.berkeley.edu/callboard) · checked 2026-08-23
Note: Exact productions-per-year count not found.

## Non-major participation

Auditions for TDPS productions are 'open to all UC Berkeley students, regardless of major,' via a two-stage process (open call then callbacks); additional opportunities exist through student film clubs and other student-run performance groups.

Source: [UC Berkeley TDPS](https://tdps.berkeley.edu/undergrad/auditions) · checked 2026-08-23
