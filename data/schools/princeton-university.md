## Mathematics major

A.B. in Mathematics, Dept. of Mathematics; 31 courses total (19+ outside the department), 8+ upper-level math courses, senior thesis based on original research. Joint Program in Applied & Computational Mathematics (PACM) also available.

Source: [Princeton Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.princeton.edu/undergraduate) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: certificate/minor (transitioning)

Program in Theater and Music Theater, Lewis Center for the Arts — historically a certificate, shifting to a minor starting with the Class of 2025. All productions open to any Princeton student as performer, crew, stage manager, or designer regardless of enrollment in the program.

Source: [Princeton Lewis Center for the Arts](https://arts.princeton.edu/programs/theater-music-theater) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Princeton Triangle Club (oldest touring student musical-comedy troupe in the US, one new original student musical/year, tours nationally); Theatre Intime (only fully student-run theater space on campus); Princeton University Players (student-run musical theater company).

Source: [Princetoniana / Triangle Club, Theatre Intime, PUP](https://princetoniana.princeton.edu/traditions/performing-arts/triangle-club) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Explicitly open to all students regardless of major or experience; PUP welcomes 'any level of theatrical experience.' 'Try On Theater Days' offer group workshops instead of traditional auditions to lower the barrier to entry.

Source: [Princeton University Players / Lewis Center for the Arts](https://pup.princeton.edu/about-us-2/) · checked 2026-08-23
