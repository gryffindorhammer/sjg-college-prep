## Mathematics major

Eberly College of Science offers a B.S. in Mathematics with six options (actuarial science, applied/industrial, computational mathematics, graduate-study track, systems analysis) plus a B.A. track; entrance to the major requires MATH 140 completed with a C or better and a 2.00 cumulative GPA.

Source: [Penn State Eberly College of Science — Mathematics Majors](https://science.psu.edu/math/undergraduate/math-major) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major (BA liberal-arts track; separately-admitted, audition-based BFA in Musical Theatre)

School of Theatre offers a liberal-arts B.A. in Theatre (pairs well with a double major/minors) alongside a highly selective, prescreen/audition-based B.F.A. in Musical Theatre — the largest dedicated musical-theatre faculty in the country, with alumni regularly working on Broadway.

Source: [Penn State School of Theatre — BFA Musical Theatre](https://arts.psu.edu/degrees/bachelor-of-fine-arts-in-theatre/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Penn State Centre Stage and OffStage seasons offer roles as assistant directors, dramaturgs, and crew; numerous student theatre groups also direct, design, and perform independently — including No Refund Theatre, a student-run, all-free organization founded in 1993 that stages a mix of established works and original student-written plays/musicals each semester.

Source: [No Refund Theatre](https://sites.psu.edu/norefundtheatrepsu/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

No Refund Theatre is explicitly open to all students "regardless of major" to perform, direct, and produce; the BA Theatre program is a liberal-arts major (no audition to declare) that many students pair with a different primary major or minor. The BFA Musical Theatre track, by contrast, is a separately-admitted, prescreen/audition-required program.

Source: [No Refund Theatre — How to Get Involved](https://sites.psu.edu/norefundtheatrepsu/how-to-get-involved/) · checked 2026-08-23
