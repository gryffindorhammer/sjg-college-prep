Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorDept. of Mathematics offers the major paired with a BA or BS; six 'named options' combine math with an applied focus area. BA requires 7 distinct courses (~21 credits) spanning at least two areas of advanced mathematics.UW-Madison Department of Mathematics / Guide · checked 2026-08-23
UW-Madison math is generally well-regarded (historically top-tier in U.S. News grad math rankings), but a current sourced figure wasn't confirmed this session.
Theater/drama programMajor and certificate — Dept. of Theatre and Drama offers a BS in Theatre and Drama (declared directly upon admission) AND a standalone Certificate in Theatre explicitly for 'any UW-Madison undergraduate student who is NOT a declared Theatre major' (16+ credits, 2.5 GPA in certificate coursework), flexible toward acting, technical theatre, costume design, or stage management.UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama / Guide · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsUniversity Theatre is UW-Madison's main student-facing producing organization within the department.UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama · checked 2026-08-23
Annual production count not confirmed.
Non-major participationMany Theatre and Drama courses are explicitly open to non-majors. University Theatre auditions are open to 'all current and incoming UW-Madison students, regardless of major.' The Theatre Certificate is a formal, credit-bearing path built specifically for non-majors.UW-Madison Department of Theatre and Drama · checked 2026-08-23
