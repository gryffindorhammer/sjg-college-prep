## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics offers a liberal-arts major requiring a minimum of 9 full courses (or equivalent), spanning introductory through applied math, statistics, and operations research; C-/P minimum in all courses counted toward the major.

Source: [Oberlin Department of Mathematics — Handbook for Majors](https://www2.oberlin.edu/math/handbook.html) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major

Dept. of Theater season includes three faculty/guest-directed mainstage productions in Wurtzel Theater plus several student-directed productions in Kander Theater; contemporary musicals (e.g. Urinetown, Cabaret) staged roughly every other year. Distinct from the Conservatory's separate, audition-admitted Musical Theater major.

Source: [Oberlin Department of Theater — Season](https://www.oberlin.edu/arts-and-sciences/departments/theater/theater-season) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Oberlin Student Theater Association (OSTA) is fully student-run, producing five fully-staged shows a year plus playwriting workshops, staged readings, and a twice-yearly 24-hour theater competition. Oberlin Musical Theater Association (OMTA) coordinates, finances, and produces student-run alternative musical theater and workshops.

Source: [The Oberlin Theater Club](https://www.oberlin.edu/blogs/oberlin-theater-club) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Casting for department productions is open to all Oberlin students regardless of year or major — the same is true of OSTA and OMTA's student-run shows, making it easy for non-majors to perform each semester.

Source: [Oberlin Department of Theater — Curriculum](https://www.oberlin.edu/arts-and-sciences/departments/theater/curriculum) · checked 2026-08-23
