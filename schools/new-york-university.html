Stephen's fit: math & theater

Mathematics majorCourant Institute's Dept. of Mathematics (College of Arts & Science) B.A. requires thirteen 4-credit courses (52 credits) at or above MATH-UA 120 with a C or better; a joint Mathematics and Computer Science B.A. is also offered. Students must earn an A in MATH-UA 325 Analysis before petitioning into graduate math courses — reflecting Courant's strong research orientation.NYU Courant — Undergraduate Major & Minor Requirements · checked 2026-08-23
Theater/drama programBFA (Tisch School of the Arts, separately/competitively admitted) — Tisch School of the Arts' Department of Drama is a conservatory-model BFA — students train through one of several affiliated acting studios; admission is competitive and separate from general NYU/CAS admission. Steinhardt's Program in Educational Theatre (outside Tisch) also produces two mainstage shows a year (fall and spring).NYU Tisch School of the Arts — Drama · checked 2026-08-23
Student productions & clubsMultiple student-run musical theater groups are open campus-wide regardless of school/major: Lamplighters (student-run spring musicals, e.g. Footloose, Bye Bye Birdie), CAST/College of Arts and Science Theater (stages the annual Broadway-parody show Forbidden NYU), and West 4th Stage Company (produces large-scale musicals each semester under NYU's Undergraduate Student Government, with auditions every semester).MEET NYU — Non-Tisch Acting Opportunities at NYU · checked 2026-08-23
Non-major participationTisch Drama's BFA is competitively admitted and not open to non-majors, but NYU's student-run theater/musical-theater clubs (Lamplighters, CAST, West 4th Stage Company, and others) are explicitly open to any NYU undergraduate regardless of school or major, with auditions held each semester.MEET NYU — No Performing Arts Major?
