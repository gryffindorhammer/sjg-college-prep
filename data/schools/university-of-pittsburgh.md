## Mathematics major

Dept. of Mathematics (Dietrich School of Arts & Sciences) offers a B.S. in Mathematics, plus B.S. tracks in Mathematics-Economics and Applied Mathematics; after the calculus sequence and three basic theoretical courses, students complete six or more upper-level courses (C or better in each, no course double-counting). Majors declared Fall 2018 or later must also complete MATH 0500 Professional Development.

Source: [Pitt Department of Mathematics — Undergraduate Degree Programs](https://www.mathematics.pitt.edu/undergraduate/undergraduate-degree-programs-and-requirements) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: major, minor, and certificate (21-credit Musical Theatre Certificate open to all students)

Dept. of Theatre Arts offers a Theatre Arts major and minor, plus a 21-credit Musical Theatre Certificate open to all students regardless of major. No audition or portfolio is required to join the program or enroll in classes.

Source: [Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Musical Theatre Certificate](https://www.play.pitt.edu/musical-theatre-certificate) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Department stages 3-5 mainstage shows a year (faculty/guest-directed) plus 3-12 lab shows directed by graduate or advanced undergraduate students.

Source: [Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Current Season](https://www.play.pitt.edu/current-season) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Auditions for all productions, including musicals, are open to majors and non-majors alike — first-years are explicitly invited to audition, and stages are open to students from any school or major across the university; non-majors can also apply as designers, dramaturgs, backstage crew, or assistant directors.

Source: [Pitt Department of Theatre Arts — Undergraduate](https://www.play.pitt.edu/undergraduate) · checked 2026-08-23
