## Mathematics major

BA/BS in Mathematics (~150 majors/year across related programs), requiring 3 terms of calculus at C+; BS requires more advanced coursework than BA. Actuarial Mathematics option available.

Source: [Rutgers Dept. of Mathematics / SAS Advising](https://sasundergrad.rutgers.edu/welcome/majors/explore/mathematics) · checked 2026-08-23

## Theater program

Type: minor

BA major and 18-credit minor in Theater via Mason Gross School of the Arts; no audition required to declare the minor. (BFA in Acting is a separate audition/conservatory-based program.)

Source: [Rutgers Mason Gross School of the Arts](https://www.masongross.rutgers.edu/degrees-programs/theater/programs/ba-minor/) · checked 2026-08-23

## Student productions & clubs

Cabaret Theatre (Douglass campus, since 1975; 4 mainstage productions/year plus an Original Play Festival, Directors' Showcase, and Rutgers Night Live), Livingston Theatre Company (3 mainstage musicals/year), and College Avenue Players.

Source: [Rutgers University news feature](https://www.rutgers.edu/news/growing-trend-toward-inclusion-arts-reflected-rutgers-student-theater-groups) · checked 2026-08-23

## Non-major participation

Livingston Theatre Company is 'open to all undergraduate students of Rutgers University, regardless of college affiliation or major' (actors, directors, designers, crew, musicians); Cabaret Theatre is similarly a general student org, and the Theater minor requires no audition.

Source: [The Livingston Theatre Company (official)](https://www.rultc.org/who-we-are) · checked 2026-08-23
